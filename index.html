AlphaTutor is an AI-powered, ultra-personalized learning platform designed to deliver high-quality, on-demand education. It combines structured test preparation for exams like the SAT, ACT, and GRE with a dynamic, user-driven engine that can generate entire micro-courses on any topic. By leveraging advanced AI and a scalable cloud architecture, AlphaTutor adapts its teaching style, pace, and content in real-time to match each learner’s unique profile, making elite-level tutoring accessible and affordable for everyone.

Our platform creates a continuous, personalized learning loop. Users can enter at any point, whether starting with an assessment, asking a question, or creating a new course from a simple idea.

1

Prompt or Assess

User enters a topic to learn or takes a diagnostic test.

2

3

Engage & Learn

Student interacts with content, takes quizzes, and asks questions.

4

Analyze & Refine

The system analyzes performance and adjusts the learning path in real-time.
